Grading Solver

1 · Site

Drop a LandXML survey, a DXF of outlines, or a saved job here

A survey sets the ground. A DXF of closed polylines comes in as pads. Without either you get a demo site.

2 · Settings

Used for new pads. Each pad carries its own batters, set under Grading.

Pad edges land on the nearest half cell, so the box volume runs a little high on a coarse grid — about 4% at 1 m, 1% at 0.25 m.

Click a corner, a string or a pad to work on it.

5 · Export

Vertex TIN gives an exact finished surface — every stake and pad edge is a real vertex, with a sparse skirt out to daylight — so no stair-stepping on the boxes. The ground and subgrade stay gridded (untick to grid the finished surface too, e.g. to tie exactly back to the volumes here). The DXF carries pad outlines with corner levels, design contours and a legend, each on its own layer.
