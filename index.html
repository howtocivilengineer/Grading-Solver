1 · Site

Drop a LandXML survey or a DXF of outlines here

A survey sets the ground. A DXF of closed polylines comes in as pads. Without either you get a demo site.

2 · Settings

Pad edges land on the nearest half cell, so the box volume runs a little high on a coarse grid — about 4% at 1 m, 1% at 0.25 m.

3 · Grading

Click a corner, a string or a pad to work on it.

4 · Export

The DXF carries pad outlines with corner levels, design contours, the cut and fill map and a legend — each on its own layer.
